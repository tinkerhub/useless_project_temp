Meet Mochi.
It has moods. You don't get to choose them.

We started with one simple observation: people wake up with completely different moods every day. So naturally, we built a chatbot that decided to have moods too.



See how Mochi happened ↓

So... what exactly is Mochi?

Mochi is an intentionally useless chatbot whose personality changes and affects how it responds to you.

Sometimes it is helpful. Sometimes it is absolutely not helpful. And sometimes it just feels like being Mochi.

How did we end up building this?

01

The Observation

People have random moods. Some days you're energetic. Some days you're sleepy. Some days you're annoyed for absolutely no reason. That got us thinking...

02

The Idea

What if a chatbot had moods too? Instead of making another chatbot that tries to be useful, we decided to make one that doesn't.

03

The Experiment

Okay... but how does Mochi decide its mood? We experimented with mood selection and how different moods should affect conversations.

04

The Personality

What should Mochi actually say? We worked on different personalities, response styles, randomness and conversational behaviour for each mood.

05

The Build

Then we actually built the thing. Frontend. Chatbot. Mood system. Responses. And several questionable decisions.

06

The Result

Meet Mochi. A chatbot that changes mood instead of solving your problems.
