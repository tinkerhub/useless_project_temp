Detection States

THREE STATES.
INFINITE POINTLESSNESS.

01

Talking

Sustained mouth movement detected over a rolling analysis window. You are apparently saying something.

02

Silent

Face detected, but speech-related mouth movement is inactive. You are being watched, but you are quiet.

03

No Face

The subject has disappeared from the camera frame. We can't judge you if we can't see you. A bold choice.

How It Works

FOUR STEPS.
ONE COMPLETELY
USELESS SYSTEM.

01

Grant Camera Access

Your browser asks. You say yes. Or no.
We don't judge.

02

AI Detects Your Face

Real-time facial landmark tracking using a browser-compatible computer-vision pipeline. 468 landmarks. Locally processed.

03

Mouth Movement Analysis

We measure lip distance and movement over time using a rolling window algorithm to determine speech-like motion.

04

Instant Reaction

The interface changes state and triggers the appropriate visual and audio response. Pointlessly.
