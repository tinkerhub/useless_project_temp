## Brand & Style

This design system channels an irreverent, tactile, community-driven makeathon documentary. It rejects the sterilized, frictionless uniformity of corporate SaaS in favor of an unapologetic independent print publication, underground DIY zine culture, and investigative project dossier. Built to chronicle the raw, frantic, creative energy of "എന്നെ കൊല്ലാണ്ട് ഇരിക്കാൻ പറ്റോ?", the experience merges physical artifacts—scanned newsprint, passport stamps, tape strips, and scribbled margin notes—with brutalist editorial hierarchy.

The aesthetic philosophy is rooted in **tactile neo-brutalism and print editorial collage**:
- **Tangible Ephemera:** Interfaces operate like physical corkboards, scrapbooks, and field folders. Polaroid-style project showcases, angled washi-tape tabs, rubber ink seals, and stamp-like tags provide palpable grit.
- **Asymmetric Tension:** Controlled disarray. While body copy adheres to disciplined newspaper columns, interactive cards, pull quotes, and visual badges break baseline grids at slight angles (±1° to ±3°).
- **Ink-on-Paper Authenticity:** Stark contrast between pure dense ink and fibrous cream paper grounds the narrative, punctuated by vivid risograph and neon accent blasts.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high-contrast print reality: heavy black ink on natural newsprint stock, ignited by electric DIY risograph tones.

### Canvas & Ground
- **Newsprint Cream (`#FAF2DE`):** Primary canvas surface, evoking warm aged newsprint and uncoated paper stock.
- **Substrate Tint (`#F5F5F5`):** Secondary container surface for layered paper sheets, offset receipts, and photo mounting boards.
- **Pure Ink Black (`#000000`):** Core structural color for all typography, rules, heavyweight borders (2px–3px), and solid offset hard shadows.

### High-Impact Risograph Accents
- **Vivid Lemon (`#FFE816`):** Primary highlight for alert notices, high-energy calls-to-action, and sticky notes.
- **Acid Lime (`#C3FF16`):** Secondary accent for active states, commit tags, and "live/running" operational markers.
- **Warm Orange (`#FD9800`):** Alert status, archival tabs, and project milestone callouts.
- **Lavender Pink (`#F79CFF`):** Editorial flourishes, pull-quote badges, and quirky author annotations.
- **Cyan Blueprint (`#34F3FF`):** Technical callouts, terminal embeds, and stamp accents.

### Application Rules
Never use subtle gray borders or blurred shadows. Boundaries are drawn with pure black ink strokes. Color accents must never be diluted with transparency; they are solid fills applied intentionally as stickers, highlighter ribbons, or stamp fills.

## Typography

The typography stages a clash between mechanical newsprint, archival monospaced terminal type, and humanistic flourish.

### Font Roles
- **Display Headlines (Anton):** Condensed, commanding, and punchy. Set in full uppercase for major manifesto titles, chapter headings, and project milestones.
- **Editorial Sub-voices (EB Garamond Italic):** Lyrical, high-contrast, classical serif reserved for reflective narrative excerpts, pull-quotes, and introspective documentary commentary.
- **Body & Structural Copy (Space Grotesk):** Modern, brutalist grotesk with idiosyncratic ink-traps that maintain clarity across dense multi-column newspaper layouts.
- **Labels, Metadata, & Stamps (Space Mono):** Strict monospaced typewriter/teletype presentation for logs, timestamps, system outputs, coordinate tags, and passport-seal stamps.

### Editorial Treatments
1. **Headline Stacking:** Large display headings are tightly kerned and leaded with near-zero margins, creating monolithic typographic posters.
2. **Annotation Layers:** Hand-penned margin remarks slant across grids at an offset, positioned alongside pull quotes to mimic editorial review proofs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an editorial newspaper broadsheet model merged with an archival scrapbook pasteboard.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column asymmetric grid with `2rem` gutters and a maximum content envelope of `1400px`. Columns are organized into irregular narrative groupings (e.g., a 3-column sticky sidebar dossier, a 6-column narrative stream, and a 3-column scrap/media column).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Content reorganizes into two major narrative channels.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `1rem` edge margins. Elements stack sequentially like a scrollable micro-zine.

### Spacing Discipline
Vertical rhythm follows strict solid steps. Sections are bounded by solid 2px black horizontal rules (`divider-rule`), separating narrative chapters with `space-xl` breathing room, while internal card components maintain compact, dense packaging (`space-sm` to `space-md`) reminiscent of classified ads and printed indexes.

## Elevation & Depth

This system outright forbids gaussian blurs, soft drop-shadows, and translucent backdrop filters. Elevation is purely tactile, physical, and graphic:

### Hardcast Offset Shadows
Depth is achieved exclusively through 100% opaque, crisp black drop-shadows with zero blur radius:
- **Paper Level 1 (Resting Cards, Notes):** `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #000000;`
- **Paper Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Sticky Stacks):** `box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px #000000;`
- **Paper Level 3 (Active Modals, Dossier Folders):** `box-shadow: 10px 10px 0px #000000;`

### Physical Fasteners & Layering
1. **Washi Tape Strips:** Elements like photos, badges, and pull quotes are anchored by semi-opaque tape overlays (semi-translucent ivory or neon bars angled across card corners with rough edges).
2. **Passport & Rubber Stamps:** Circular and rectangular badges sit at rotated angles (-4deg to +8deg) directly atop content cards, featuring distressed dashed borders (`border: 2px dashed #000000`) to evoke hand-applied red-tape bureaucracy.
3. **Layered Paper Stacks:** Stacked cards simulate multiple overlapping documents by revealing an offset border and secondary background beneath the top sheet.

## Shapes

The geometric personality is sharp (`roundedness: 0`), clean-cut, and industrial, reflecting sheared cardstock, trimmed paper sheets, and razor-cut collage clippings.

### Edge Specifications
- **Cards, Buttons, and Containers:** Strict 0px radius (`rounded-none`). Every element finishes with a sheared, straight guillotine edge.
- **Enclosures & Tags:** Clipped corners (chamfered at 45° by 8px) are deployed for folder tabs, ticket stubs, and tear-away coupon metadata.
- **Rubber Stamps:** Perfect circles or sharp pill outlines constructed with high-contrast outlines (2px solid black) represent the sole curved forms in the system, referencing mechanical notary seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid risograph background (Vivid Lemon `#FFE816` or Acid Lime `#C3FF16`), pure black 2px border, sharp 0px corners, bold monospaced label in all-caps. Casts a `3px 3px 0px #000000` hard shadow. On hover, shifts 2px down and right (`transform: translate(2px, 2px)`) with shadow reducing to `1px 1px 0px #000000`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Cream `#FAF2DE` background with 2px black border and black text. On hover, fills completely black with white lettering.
- **Destructive Action:** Solid Pure Ink Black background, white bold text, with a danger hazard border or bright orange `#FD9800` accent tab.

### Cards & Dossier Files
- **Standard Dossier Card:** Substrate Tint background (`#F5F5F5`), 2px solid black outline, `4px 4px 0px #000000` hard shadow. Card headers feature a tabbed folder protrusion along the top border containing the document index code in `label-code`.
- **Polaroid Showcase Card:** White border with an exaggerated 32px bottom chin. Carries high-contrast photography or project captures, finished with a monospaced handwritten-style caption. Mounted with a tilted tape strip graphic across the top center.

### Tags, Chips & Status Stamps
- **Rubber Stamp Seal:** Circular or pill-shaped container, rotated by -5°, with a 2px distressed border. Displays capitalized monospaced status words ("VERIFIED", "SURVIVED", "REJECTED", "DEPLOYED") in ink black, cyan `#34F3FF`, or orange `#FD9800`.
- **Zine Tag:** Rectangular chip, solid black fill with neon yellow text, or neon fill with black text. No border-radius. Padding: `2px 8px`.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs:** Sharp-cornered input boxes, thick 2px black borders, warm cream fill. Placeholder text styled as a faint typewriter print. On focus, triggers an Acid Lime outline glow or an offset hard shadow with zero blur.
- **Checkboxes:** Square boxes (`18px x 18px`) with 2px solid black border. Selected state is marked by an unapologetic, thick handwritten "X" or solid black block fill.
- **Radio Buttons:** Square containers hosting a centered solid mini-square when active, abandoning traditional soft circular radios to preserve brutalist integrity.

### Lists & Activity Logs
- **Ledger Rows:** Alternating row stripes using Cream (`#FAF2DE`) and Substrate Tint (`#F5F5F5`), separated by 1px solid black rules. Left column anchors a strict monospace timestamp (`HH:MM:SS`), followed by high-density event details and collaborator tags.

### Narrative Pull Quotes & Accents
- **Manifesto Banner:** Full-width block wrapped in a 3px black border, washed in Lavender Pink (`#F79CFF`) or Vivid Lemon (`#FFE816`), featuring large italic serif pull-quotes interspersed with punchy display headers.
