### Project Description
The world's most unnecessarily serious, Kerala-inspired banana ripeness computer-vision application in retro 8-bit / 16-bit arcade aesthetics. Upload any banana photograph to determine its diplomatic citizenship, legal fate in banana court, Amma approval rating, and exact remaining lifespan.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Humanity has landed rovers on Mars and sequenced genomes, yet whenever a Malayali looks at a banana on the dining table, pure existential panic ensues. Is it ripe enough for morning puttu? Will Amma throw a sandal if it's left uneaten for another 4 hours? Is it destined for glorious Pazham Pori or criminal negligence? Bananas have suffered in silence on kitchen tables for centuries without due legal process or basic diplomatic recognition.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We engineered the **Pazhamness Meter**: an AI-powered computer-vision courtroom, passport issuing authority, and maternal verdict engine. Users drop a banana photo into Minion Bob's custody; our optical segmentation pipeline analyzes yellowness quotient, sugar spot density, and chlorophyll breakdown. It then issues an official Kerala Pazham Passport, stages a criminal trial with prosecution and defense, and calculates Amma's concurrence percentage.

## Technical Details

### Technologies/Components Used
For Software:
- **Languages:** JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3, Node.js
- **Frontend Framework:** React 18, Vite
- **Styling & UI:** TailwindCSS, Retro Arcade Pixel Fonts (Press Start 2P), Custom 16-bit Pixel Art
- **Audio Engine:** Web Audio API (procedural NES square-wave sound synthesizer)
- **Backend Framework:** Express.js
- **Deployment:** Vercel (Edge CDN) & Render (Full-Stack Unified Service)
