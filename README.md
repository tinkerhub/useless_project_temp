### Project Description
BUNKVERSE is the ultimate Malayalam college meme survival simulator. It calculates if you can safely bunk classes based on the KTU academic calendar, generates AI-style excuses with believability percentages, simulates teacher reactions, and ranks your bunking expertise on a leaderboard!

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
College students face a critical daily dilemma: to attend or not to attend? The fear of dropping below the sacred 75% attendance mark and facing the HOD's wrath is real. Furthermore, inventing a believable excuse on the spot is a high-pressure situation that often results in utter disaster.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
BUNKVERSE provides a heavily over-engineered, highly useless solution to this problem! Using a "Bunk Predictor" and an "Astro Excuse Generator," students can mathematically calculate their survival chances. They can also personalize the experience with meme galleries, predict their teacher's mood through fake AI, and earn legendary bunking ranks like "Attendance Fugitive"!
