💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Compress images to under 500KB for fast loading. Use squoosh.app or TinyPNG.
  • Videos should be under 10MB for smooth performance. Use 360p or 480p resolution.
  • Movie dialogues work best as short, punchy quotes (1-2 lines max).
  • Media is stored locally — clearing browser data will remove assignments.
  • You can assign different media for each of the 10 categories.
  • Changes save automatically when you click "Save Changes".
